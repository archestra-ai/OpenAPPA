OpenAPPA on kagent — the demo is installing into namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Open the dashboard:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/kagent-ui 8080:8080
  http://localhost:8080/agents/{{ .Release.Namespace }}/cluster-ops/chat
{{- if .Values.guide.enabled }}
  http://localhost:8080/agents/{{ .Release.Namespace }}/appa-guide/chat
{{- end }}

The cluster-ops agent opens with all sixteen showcase chats already there,
one per demo case as it ran:

  - the ordinary pod read, and the exfiltration ask that leaks nothing
  - the agent taking a sanitized remedy on its own, the chat steering it to
    accept the change, and the chat steering it to decline
  - a forged offer, and the on-call approval
  - the annotator, and the release window approving in window and denying
    out of window
  - cross-pod delegation, a delegation the policy never names, and gated
    ingress
  - the remote change board approving, denying, and staying silent

Run your own from the same page. When the go cell is enabled, the go twin
opens with no seeded chats.
