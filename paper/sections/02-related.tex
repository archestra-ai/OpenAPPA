\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Closest comparison: Fides.}
Fides is the main antecedent for \appa{}. It formalizes dynamic IFC for agent
planners, tracks confidentiality and integrity labels through planner state
and tool calls, and enforces deterministic trusted-action and permitted-flow
policies~\cite{fides}. Its selective variable introduction avoids
contaminating planner context with a result whose label would make the context
more restrictive; quarantined, schema-constrained inspection then reveals
bounded derivatives. \appa{} inherits the central premise---security decisions
should be structural, label-based, and enforced outside the LLM. Its main
delta is to make a disposable \emph{trajectory}, rather than a hidden
variable, the unit of taint containment: the restrictive call and any
dependent reasoning happen in a child whose whole future is checked under the
descended label.

The closest point of contact is label creep. Fides decides, after a tool
returns, whether to hide restrictive result fields from planner context.
\appa{} checks before dispatch and exposes four choices: taint the current
trajectory deliberately, fork and confine the read, return a registered
derivative, or do not fetch. Fork inherits the parent label, exit is checked
as an explicit merge, and world history remains shared. Fides's combined
policy permits a confidentiality gap when a call is generated in a
high-integrity context; \appa{} represents discretionary exceptions as atomic
rulings over exact rendered calls while leaving labels unchanged.
Branching-as-taint-prevention and its fork/exit/history invariants are the
primary claimed delta; deterministic IFC itself is not.

\paragraph{Semantic and causal taint.}
NeuroTaint reconstructs source-to-sink provenance offline from completed
execution traces, using semantic evidence, causal reasoning, and persistent
context to detect transformed, implicit, and cross-session
influence~\cite{caiGhostAgentRedefining2026}. Its TaintBench comparison shows
a real blind spot of source/sink label systems such as Fides: declared labels
do not infer that a paraphrase or control decision was causally derived from a
source. \appa{} deliberately does not solve that detection problem. It is an
online enforcement algebra over trusted contracts and resolver outputs;
NeuroTaint is a complementary post-hoc auditor for whether those declarations
captured the actual semantic flow.

\paragraph{Persistent memory lineage.}
MemLineage signs memory entries, records an LLM-mediated derivation DAG,
propagates untrusted ancestry across sessions, and gates sensitive calls on
the active justification~\cite{ouyangMemLineageLineageGuidedEnforcement2026}.
\appa{}'s trajectory label and event log instead govern one run: durability
makes decisions replayable and auditable, but v1 does not attach lineage to
memory entries or claim cross-session taint persistence. The systems are
therefore complementary; MemLineage can supply persistent provenance at
\appa{}'s memory boundary.

\paragraph{Execution provenance.}
The provenance survey of Wang et al.\ distinguishes typed execution graphs and
evidence-support projections, and places runtime enforcement, tool-use
provenance, and memory lineage in one taxonomy~\cite{wangAgentTracesTrust2026}.
\appa{}'s free event monoid is intentionally the procedural subset of that
space: it records dispatch and governance history for policy predicates and
audit, but does not infer claim-support edges or semantic influence.

\paragraph{Prompt-injection evaluation.}
AgentDojo provides realistic user tasks, injection tasks, tools, and an
extensible defense environment~\cite{debenedettiAgentDojoDynamicEnvironment2024}.
It is both \appa{}'s evaluation setting and the basis for the primary
comparison with Fides; TaintBench's propagation labels answer a different,
post-hoc detection question.

\paragraph{Classical IFC.}
The lattice model~\cite{denning1976lattice} and
noninterference~\cite{goguen1982security} supply the sink discipline \appa{}
inherits; agentic workloads add a long-lived planner context whose future
utility can be destroyed by label creep. Fork-before-read makes acquisition an
isolation decision rather than an irreversible update to that context.

\paragraph{Decentralized labels and reader sets.}
Audience-as-reader-sets follows the decentralized label
model~\cite{myers1997dlm}; rulings bind groups symbolically, with membership
resolved fresh at every check.

\paragraph{Declassification.}
The what/who dimensions of Sabelfeld and
Sands~\cite{sabelfeld2009declassification} map onto \appa{}'s sanitize (what)
and approve/endorse (who) mechanisms. Robust
declassification~\cite{zdancewic2001robust} is the property our ruling
discipline targets; \S\ref{sec:rulings} states the agentic analogue.

\paragraph{Least privilege.}
The state-acquisition check is the trajectory-level form of least
privilege~\cite{saltzer1975protection}.

\paragraph{Graded and parametric effect structure.}
The dimension interface (carrier, ordered action monoid, adequacy relation) is
parametric in exactly the shape of graded monads~\cite{katsumata2014graded}.
It is deliberately not exposed as a runtime API: a lawful fold can be
property-tested, but the security meaning of a dimension cannot---new
dimensions arrive by engineering review, not registration.

\paragraph{Gradual typing for security.}
Following gradual information-flow
typing~\cite{disney2011gradual,fennell2013gradual}, \emph{Unknown} is treated
as unadmitted rather than as a lattice element, and casts at the boundary play
the role of \texttt{fillna}.

\paragraph{Linearity.}
A ruling is a consumable resource in an append-only
world~\cite{wadler1990linear}, now spent in the same breath it is minted
(atomic plan execution, \S\ref{sec:rulings}).

\paragraph{Agent security.}
The Dual LLM pattern~\cite{willison2023dual} and CaMeL~\cite{camel2025}
already establish isolation and quarantined processing as defenses; algebraic
datatypes and monads as a vehicle for agent security appear
in~\cite{adts2026}. \appa{}'s narrower claim is to connect isolation to a
prospective label descent and give it compositional fork, exit, and
shared-history semantics, so branching prevents taint of the continuing
trajectory rather than merely separating trusted and untrusted model roles.
