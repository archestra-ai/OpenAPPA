\section{Limitations and Future Work}\label{sec:limitations}

Benign-but-confusable is a load-bearing assumption: covert channels from a malicious model are out of scope, and trusted-source malice is out of scope by definition.

Branching requires a confining runtime. A gateway that cannot prevent child bytes from entering the parent context cannot implement the main contribution, whatever labels it attaches afterward. Copying context also incurs token, latency, and state-management costs that the evaluation must expose.

Taint prevention is parent-local, not transactional rollback. A child may perform admitted mutations or egresses in the world; abandonment discards only its context and candidate return value. The shared log makes those effects visible to policy but cannot undo them.

Safe branch exit is only as strong as the registered derivation. Schema validation cannot prove absence of semantic influence, and a quarantine-exit attestation belongs to the TCB. Branching shrinks where taint can propagate; it does not eliminate the classical sanitizer-correctness problem.

Contract labels are declared provenance, not semantic influence inference. APPA does not detect paraphrase, implicit control dependence, or cross-session laundering as NeuroTaint does~\cite{caiGhostAgentRedefining2026}, nor does v1 persist signed derivation lineage with memory as MemLineage does~\cite{ouyangMemLineageLineageGuidedEnforcement2026}. Those mechanisms can inform resolver inputs or audit APPA traces, but they are outside the core algebra.

Approval fatigue is the real attack surface of any HITL scheme and an out-of-scope adoption concern: the algebra is only as good as the authorities and contracts registered into it.

\fixme{Dispatch atomicity---one open ruling---concerns the crash gap between invoking a tool and appending its events. Option (a) is a durable outbox: invocation and events commit as one durable record before the invoke. This is a storage-layer clause, invisible to contract authors, and the log stays \emph{true} (the recommendation on record). Option (b) is polarity-split events (\texttt{attempted}/\texttt{done}; negatives gate on attempted, positives on done). This fails closed both ways but reintroduces the pre/post axis into every contract and leaves permanent conservative lies in the log.}

\fixme{Parked: outcome-sensitive completion events; branch-scoped \texttt{prior(k)} (a steered quarantined child can mint a parent-gate event with a real, harmless dispatch).}

\paragraph{Epoch-wide raise (expressible, not taken).}
The ruling-carried persistent widening is evicted by the next narrowing. The extension is algebraically coherent and comes as one package---the permissive action, non-commutative folds, the keep-then-add collapse (Proposition~\ref{prop:collapse}), eviction soundness (\S\ref{sec:enforcement})---plus a request-channel design (contract annotation versus a second offered plan versus harness configuration). We state a preference instead of deferring: for the applied problem---an ongoing exchange with an external recipient---\emph{branching is the better construction}. The child confines the recipient-specific exchange and any restrictive observations, call-scoped rulings admit only the reviewed sends, and the child then dies. \texttt{merge}-by-intersection prevents silent leak-back, so APPA keeps labels narrow-only and spends its complexity budget on branch isolation and checked exits.

The honest caveat is that deployments will be tempted to amortize review with auto-approving authorities. Such an authority must compare each rendered call against the one it reviewed---recreating epoch behavior without that check is strictly worse than the explicit extension would have been.

Further work includes mandate scoping by information type; pass-by-reference labels, where byte-identical unretyped arguments keep their own label (this falls out of arguments being LabeledValues, but is deferred); and partial-context handoff, in which a child is forked with a sanitized subset of the parent's context while retaining the full shared event history, which is currently out of scope.

Dimensions must be vendor-shipped after engineering review, never merely registered: lawfulness is property-testable, but security meaning is not. Candidate dimensions include the spend-budget monoid as the canonical case (ordered, per-run, and accumulating in the free direction---today a log view summed by a resolver-implemented authority), independent attestations, and value-specific purpose limitation.
