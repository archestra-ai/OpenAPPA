The OpenAPPA Advisory Board brings together engineers and researchers from different companies to look at the project from the outside. Members read the specification, comment on proposals, and review changes as they land. The role is advisory: the board shapes the project through argument, not through authority.

The board is under formation, and the seats above are filling. If you build agents, secure them, or study them — and you want to watch a security model get argued out in the open — write to [matvey@archestra.ai](mailto:matvey@archestra.ai) with a line about where you work and what you would poke at first. An email is enough; there is no form and no obligation beyond the reading you feel like doing.
